Delete Your Account

You can request the deletion of your WodScan account and all associated data at any time.

What gets deleted:

  • Your workout history and scores
  • Your personal records and statistics
  • Your leaderboard entries

How to request deletion:

Send an email to the address below with the subject "Account Deletion Request", including the email address associated with your account.
