How it works

THREE STEPS.
ZERO EFFORT.

No more typing your WOD manually. No more forgotten PRs. WodScan does the work so you can focus on training.

01
📸
Photograph the board

Walk in, take a photo of the whiteboard. That's literally it. One tap, no typing, no squinting at your notes later.

→
02
🤖
AI reads everything

Claude AI extracts the WOD — type, movements, loads, rounds — and ignores the warm-up and skill sections automatically.

Enter your time or score in one tap. Your result is saved, ranked on the leaderboard, and compared to your history.
